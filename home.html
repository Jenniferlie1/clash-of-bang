About Clash of BaNG

Clash of BaNG is a fantasy gaming community founded in 2018 by strategy game enthusiasts who share a passion for Dark Troops, hero tactics, and clan battles. Originally started as a small online forum, the community has grown into regional hubs across Jakarta Enclave, Bandung Highlands, and Sanur Outpost, providing troop guides, fan artwork, beginner tutorials, and clan activities for players around the world.
